PROMPT 1 - save as: figs/fig_pertanyaan_pimpinan.png
Slide: "Tiga Pertanyaan Pimpinan Setiap Pagi"
Ukuran: 4:3 landscape, ideal 1536 x 1152 px
================================================================================

Create a detailed editorial vector infographic illustration for an Indonesian
regional-government presentation. Use a clean white background, confident navy
linework, subtle pale-blue environmental details, restrained soft shadows, and
small gold focal accents. Match a polished institutional annual-report style:
richly composed and informative, but not photorealistic, not a flat icon set,
and not a slide mockup.

Scene:
An Indonesian regional-government leader wearing an elegant navy-and-white
batik shirt begins an early-morning briefing at a tidy desk. The leader is
looking attentively at a tablet. Include a small cup of coffee, a desk clock
showing early morning, a folder of briefing papers, and a softly drawn Kulon
Progo small-town landscape in the background: low hills, rice fields, public
buildings, and a warm rising sun. The setting should feel calm, responsible,
and ready for action.

From the tablet, three clearly separated rounded question cards rise in a
gentle upward flow. Give each card a navy outline, white interior, a small
gold number badge, and one simple supporting symbol:
  1. a pulse or alert symbol for the current situation;
  2. a severity gauge for seriousness;
  3. a handoff/checklist symbol for action and responsibility.

Render these exact Indonesian captions as large, legible text:
  "Apa yang sedang terjadi?"
  "Seberapa serius?"
  "Apa yang harus dilakukan?"
  "Siapa yang menangani?"

Place the third and fourth captions together inside the third card, with a
clear line break. On the tablet, show only an abstract monitoring dashboard
with small charts, issue cards, and one tiny rounded tile containing the letter
"P". Do not show a real software interface or any real company logo.

Color palette:
  - Deep UGM navy #01416B for outlines, batik details, text, and interface lines
  - Gold #FDD402 for the sunrise, number badges, and limited focal highlights
  - Pale navy #E6EEF3 and light blue-grey tints for background context/shading
  - White #FFFFFF for the canvas and card interiors
  - PANTAU teal #0E7C86 to mint #2FD4C4 only on the tiny "P" dashboard tile

Do not introduce strong red, green, purple, or orange. No photorealism, no
brand logos, no social-media logos, no watermark, no illegible filler text,
no extra fingers, and no large decorative title.

Create a warm, detailed editorial vector illustration for the closing slide of
an Indonesian university-and-government presentation. Use a clean white
background, confident navy linework, subtle pale-blue environmental details,
restrained soft shadows, and a bright but limited gold focal accent. Match a
polished institutional annual-report style: richly composed, optimistic, and
human, but not photorealistic, not a flat icon set, and not a slide mockup.

Scene:
At the center, an Indonesian university researcher and a Kulon Progo regional
government official stand side by side at a shared table. They are collaborating
as equal partners, looking at a monitoring dashboard on a screen between them.
Show natural professional gestures: one person points to an emerging issue on
the screen while the other holds a short action note. Add a subtle group of
local public-service representatives in the midground and a softly illustrated
Kulon Progo landscape behind them: Menoreh hills, rice fields, a road, public
buildings, and residents going about daily life.

Create a clear visual metaphor for faster government response:
  - A bright gold signal line begins at several small citizen speech bubbles,
    reaches the monitoring screen, and then continues rapidly to a gold action
    check mark near the government team.
  - Behind it, a wider pale-navy ripple representing a spreading public issue

  - The gold signal must visibly arrive first.

Render this exact Indonesian phrase once, clearly and prominently on a small
navy-and-white ribbon integrated into the illustration:

On the dashboard screen, show abstract charts, issue cards, and one tiny rounded
tile containing the letter "P". Do not use actual application screenshots,
real institutional seals, or real company logos. The final mood should convey
trust, public service, partnership, readiness, and forward movement rather than
a commercial sales pitch.

Color palette:
  - Deep UGM navy #01416B for figures, outlines, text, and the slower issue ripple
  - Gold #FDD402 for the fast signal, action check mark, and focal highlights
  - Pale navy #E6EEF3 and light blue-grey tints for landscape/context/shading
  - White #FFFFFF for the canvas and negative space
  - PANTAU teal #0E7C86 to mint #2FD4C4 only on the tiny "P" dashboard tile

Do not introduce strong red, green, purple, or orange. No photorealism, no
brand logos, no watermark, no illegible filler text, no extra fingers, no
