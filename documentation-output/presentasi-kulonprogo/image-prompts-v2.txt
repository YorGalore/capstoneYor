Slide: "Dua Jalur Pengumpulan Data"
Ukuran: mendekati persegi/4:3, ideal 1400 x 1400 px
================================================================================

Create a detailed editorial vector infographic illustration for an Indonesian
regional-government presentation about a public-monitoring system. Use a
clean white background, confident navy linework, subtle pale-blue
environmental shading, restrained soft shadows, and small gold focal
accents. Match a polished institutional annual-report style: richly
composed and informative, not a flat icon set, not photorealistic, and not
a software mockup.

Scene:
Show two parallel data-collection pipelines converging into one central
system. On the left, a pipeline begins at an abstract rounded
social-app tile (a generic chat-bubble icon, no real platform logo) and
flows through a small gateway shape labeled with the caption "API Resmi".
On the right, a second pipeline begins at an abstract stack of newspaper /
online-news-article icons (folded paper with text lines and a small
headline mark, representing "portal berita daring") and flows through a
spider/crawler-web icon labeled "Penelusuran Web". Both pipelines pass
through a shared funnel-shaped filter labeled "Kata kunci & wilayah" before
arriving at a small rounded server/database icon that carries one tiny
teal-to-mint gradient tile with the letter "P" on it. Add a small shield or
padlock icon near the funnel labeled "Data publik saja" to convey privacy
and compliance. Keep the whole composition calm, orderly, and legible, with
soft pale-blue circuit-like flow lines connecting the stages left to right.

Render these exact Indonesian captions as large, legible text:
  "API Resmi"
  "Penelusuran Web"
  "Portal Berita Daring"
  "Kata kunci & wilayah"
  "Data publik saja"

Color palette:
  - Deep UGM navy #01416B for outlines, icons, flow lines, and captions
  - Gold #FDD402 for the filter funnel accent and small highlight marks
  - Pale navy #E6EEF3 and light blue-grey tints for background context
  - White #FFFFFF for the canvas and card interiors
  - PANTAU teal #0E7C86 to mint #2FD4C4 only on the tiny "P" database tile

Do not introduce strong red, green, purple, or orange. No photorealism, no
real brand or social-media logos, no watermark, no illegible filler text,
no extra fingers, and no large decorative title.
